A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you contribute

Is this a real payment button?

Yes. The supplied button uses live PayPal, not a sandbox. This page is for review only. You may inspect the payment options and checkout entry, but do not log in, authorize, or complete a payment during testing.

Is this a charitable donation?

No. This is voluntary reader support for an independent publishing project, not a charitable fundraiser. No charitable tax receipt is offered.

Does support influence the research?

Support does not buy coverage, favorable conclusions, investment advice, or a promised return. Editorial decisions remain independent. Any sponsored or commissioned work will be labeled separately.

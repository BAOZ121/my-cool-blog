Version: review figures 1.0. Prepared 10 October 2026.

Global municipal solid waste pathways 2022

The source is World Bank, What a Waste 3.0 (2026), Figure 2.16, printed page 29 / PDF page 65 [G02]. Source landing page: https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/what-a-waste . Original report: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031926140025768/pdf/P501986-ef2864e0-591b-4258-9e00-75af80be0e56.pdf . The seven original integer shares are used directly: uncollected 17%, recycling 15%, composting and anaerobic digestion 6%, incineration with energy recovery 20%, sanitary landfill 17%, controlled landfill 12%, dumpsite 13%. Sum: 100%. Each SVG segment has an angle of its published percentage multiplied by 3.6 degrees. No shares were normalized, no missing category was invented, and no absolute tonnes or additional decimal precision were reverse calculated.

The denominator is global municipal solid waste generated by mass, in the World Bank's modeled 2022 baseline. National observations are mostly from 2020-2024, harmonized and weighted by 2022 generation; this is not a simultaneous census of every plant. The figure is not company revenue share, all-solid-waste market share, or a yield of saleable recovered output. Uncollected waste is its own published category; self-managed routes must not be added as extra slices.

The PNG ring is rendered by the standard Windows .NET System.Windows.Forms.DataVisualization.Charting library. Bundled Pillow composes the English labels, title, scope, units and source note. The editable SVG uses mathematical annular sector paths from the same integer data, with all text and fills editable. PNG and SVG encode the same percentages; their engine-specific antialiasing or ring placement may differ. Data and classification order are in msw_routes_2022.json and figures/msw_pathways_2022_data.csv. Source licence: CC BY 3.0 IGO. The figure carries an adaptation disclaimer and does not imply World Bank endorsement.

Global solid waste value chain

The four columns encode industry -> upstream / midstream / downstream -> segment -> representative company or product. Content comes directly from en_market.json figure_chain. The classification cites C01-C09, H06, H08, H09 and H12; the Supporting Evidence appendix records each source's identity and limitations. The diagram classifies roles as of 10 October 2026, not confirmed supplier contracts or a ranked market share. Companies may participate in multiple stages. No physical or financial quantities are calculated from the diagram.

The PNG is 3000 x 1750 pixels, using a light background, muted beige nodes and dark blue text. All diagram labels and footnotes are at least 40 pixels; the SVG preserves the same four-level grouping, text, connectors and source note. At the DOCX figure width of 244 mm, 40-pixel text is approximately 9.2 points. The standalone PNG and SVG retain their full resolution. Bundled Pillow draws the diagram using installed Arial fonts. Use the editable SVG and JSON to modify node content and the task-local tools/build_figures.py to regenerate both figure pairs. The ring renderer is tools/build_donut.ps1. These scripts do not create or edit DOCX files and do not install packages.
